Modifications by MEngine contributors: assembled bearded chainmail infantry
with a hood and animated cape; baked the musket's procedural wood/metal
materials to PBR textures; attached the gun to the right-hand weapon bone;
trimmed the crossbow clip to the aiming, firing and recovery segment;
baked combined atlases and exported skeletal glTF poses. This clip does not
depict a complete historical muzzle-loading sequence.
