The RealFootman and RealWorker models, their textures and materials, and the
derived combined Assets/Art/unit-portraits.png atlas are distributed under
CC-BY-SA-3.0. Other original character art in the atlas remains available
separately under its original license as documented in the sample manifests.

Modifications by MEngine contributors: assembled body, head and equipment;
blue cloth and brown hair masks; simplified oval shield; baked UV atlases;
specular-to-roughness conversion; four-weight skinning; skeletal animation
conversion; normalized scale; native-rendered portraits.
RealFootman uses Briton chainmail swordsman modules. RealWorker combines
official male tunic, Celtic head, axe and biped animations.
