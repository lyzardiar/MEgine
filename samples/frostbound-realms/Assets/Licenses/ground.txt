Original diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and displacement sources are pinned
with URLs and SHA-256 in ground-sources.json. Derived textures pack normal X/Y,
roughness and relative height into linear RGBA; no gamma correction is applied.
Generated by scripts/import-frost-ground.py using Pillow and numpy.
Ground.mmat binds cliff color as base_color_texture and cliff linear NRH as
metallic_roughness_texture. Ground.mshader projects both onto vertical faces and
supplies the final PBR channels; the packed data is not a standard ORM texture.

The soil/grass color and NRH atlases preserve both scans with wrapped gutters.
The cliff color and NRH atlases retain rock_face_03, Ice001 and castle_brick_07
with wrapped gutters; each tile selects its own source scan panel.
