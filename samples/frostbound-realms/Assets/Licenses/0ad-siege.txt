RealBallista, RealCatapult, RealTrebuchet and their Shoot variants, RealRam,
their textures and materials, and the combined Assets/Art/unit-portraits.png
atlas are distributed under CC-BY-SA-3.0. Other original art in the atlas
is available separately under the licenses documented in the sample manifests.

Modifications by MEngine contributors: assembled machinery and ammunition;
baked base color, normal and roughness atlases; converted and normalized glTF
meshes and sampled mechanical animations; attached loaded projectiles to bones;
assembled two ballista operators and four traction trebuchet operators with
their heads, helmets, uniforms, levers and synchronized skeletal animations.
The traction trebuchet uses the artillery stone mesh with granite texture.
Artillery movement uses the idle mechanical pose with walking operators;
the ram retains its authored movement clip. The onager and ram have no visible crew.
Pinned source URLs and source/generated hashes: siege-sources.json.
