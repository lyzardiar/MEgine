Adaptation by MiYu / MEngine: selected static Gallic and Briton architecture;
combined authored structural props; normalized bounds; composed tower upgrades;
baked original color, normal and occlusion textures into new UV atlases; converted
specular intensity to roughness; baked player-color regions as brown cloth;
converted Collada geometry to glTF; rendered the game building portrait atlas.
Source actors, models, textures and hashes are retained in the source project
under SourceAssets/0ad and warclans-sources.json.
