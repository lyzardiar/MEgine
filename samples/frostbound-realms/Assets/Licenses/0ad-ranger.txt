Modifications by MEngine contributors: assembled a female archer body, long
hair, bow, quiver, arrows and animated cape; applied green cloth and brown
hair colors; baked base, normal and ARM atlases; converted normalized glTF
meshes and skeletal animations. Bow limbs and string retain their authored
animation; separate hand-held, nocked and released arrow states follow the
combat/skill timeline. Source meshes and downloaded textures are unchanged.
