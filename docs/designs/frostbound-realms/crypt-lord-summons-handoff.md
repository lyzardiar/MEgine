# Crypt Lord permanent summons and burrow handoff

Author: MiYu

## Delivered scope

`cryptLordVersion: 1` adds authoritative Carrion Beetles alongside the existing Impale/Spiked Carapace foundation. Default stays 0 and protocol stays 73 until Locust Swarm and product presentation are integrated.

- Manual and autocast AUcb choose a nearby visible usable corpse. The source hero cast point delays corpse consumption, mana payment and cooldown until release; missing/claimed corpses or occupied capacity leave resources intact. Autocast preserves ordinary movement/combat orders.
- Source ucs1/ucs2/ucs3 use ranked health, melee dice, windup, collision, armor and sight. Summons are permanent, require no food or mana, regenerate on blight and survive caster death. At five beetles, another successful release replaces the oldest, including when the global unit capacity is full. Replacement awards no bounty and leaves no corpse.
- Abu2/Abu3 use source ucsB/ucsC forms after their 1.45-second timer (rounded to the 0.1-second simulation step). Burrowed beetles are invisible except to their owner and detection, have no weapon or movement, and regenerate anywhere. Unburrow restores the base profile.
- Morphs remain uninterruptible. Shared attack eligibility prevents Mountain Giant Taunt from changing a transforming beetle's order. Town Portal may reset the arrival order while the morph timer continues; saves retain deterministic continuation.
- Strict save validation checks source identity, ranked stats, permanent-summon provenance, maximum count, forms and timers. Public state removes summoner/birth and morph internals, exposing only presentation fields. Actual TCP verifies ownership, hidden enemy projection, reconnect and revelation on unburrow.
