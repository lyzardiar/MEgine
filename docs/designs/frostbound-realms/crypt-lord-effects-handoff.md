# Crypt Lord source effects handoff

Author: MiYu

## Delivered scope

`cryptLordVersion: 1` renders the imported Impale ground spikes, hit and overhead stun art, four animated Spiked Carapace attachments and the Crypt Lord's sampled embedded effects. New skirmish games use version 1 and protocol 74; missing-version legacy saves retain version 0. Default activation and current two-client evidence are documented in `crypt-lord-default-handoff.md`.

- The hero importer preserves all 139 original nodes and 15 attachment locators in each of the eight body GLBs. It extends the original hierarchy and animation/attachment visibility tracks while retaining geometry, skin indices and binary buffers. The Undead art importer extends the signed Crypt Lord outputs after source conversion; other hero outputs and the shared importer remain unchanged.
- BUts attachments use the original Chest Left/Right and Chest Mount Left/Right matrices, including rotation, world scale and KATV visibility. Learned, living, untransformed heroes display the attachments. BUim's imported hit and stun art follows Sprite First/Overhead anchors with the existing origin fallback where a target has no named locator.
- Short-lived authoritative `impaleTrails` retain ground visuals after the 0.3-second damage wave ends and after caster death. Ground spikes appear at one-unit intervals over the six-unit wave and play their original 1.4-second Birth animation. The spacing is a presentation choice, not measured original-runtime behavior.
- Save validation checks source, rank, age, normalized direction, bounds, duplicate identities and active-wave correspondence. Legacy saves without the new field reconstruct active trails. Public state hides complete trail records and sends only already-born, individually visible spike positions, birth frames and yaw.
- A twelve-slot initial ground-effect pool expands within the wave-derived bound, waits for asynchronous registration, establishes mesh parent relationships, reuses slots and hides expired effects. The generated scene now contains 89853 entities and 416 model entries.
