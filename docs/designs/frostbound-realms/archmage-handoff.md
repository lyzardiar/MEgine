# Original Human Archmage

Author: MiYu

New skirmishes use the original `Hamg` for the first Human hero. Its original model, source portrait camera, INT damage, attributes, regeneration, attack timing, B/W/R/T skill cards and research positions are integrated into the generated native client and authoritative simulation. Legacy saves without `archmageVersion` retain their previous Human hero rules. Protocol 61 includes projectile age for original sampled missile animation on remote clients.

`import-frost-archmage.py` reproduces 598 signed outputs: ten original geometry bindings, two portrait cameras, seven sampled effect definitions, original icons, source tables, source AI skill order and conversion receipts. Both FireBallMissile geometry and metadata and the particle-only WaterElementalMissile are retained. Original Blizzard asset terms apply; the source archive precedence, bytes and hashes are recorded in `archmage-sources.json`. Independent conversion produces identical files and receipt. `asset-library` remains unchanged.

Blizzard uses the source rank wave counts, damage, total per-wave limit, building factor, radius and one-second wave field. It affects eligible allied and enemy ground units and excludes invulnerable, airborne and magic-immune targets. Friendly deaths do not award kill gold or deny experience. Stop, movement, stun, Cyclone, death and transport clear the channel. Rank and pending wave timers survive saving.

Water Elemental uses original rank HP 525/675/900, average piercing damage 20/35/45, anti-air weapon, source collision and model scales .9/1.1/1.3. Summons last 60 seconds, use deterministic nearby free space and preserve rank in missile snapshots. Brilliance grants eligible allied mana users the strongest nearby source aura bonus .75/1.5/2.25 per second; it does not manufacture mana for Water Elementals. Mass Teleport accepts visible allied ground units/buildings and eligible passive neutral destinations, channels for three seconds and places up to 24 ground passengers including the caster beside the current destination position, reserving collision space.

Source, simulation, generated-client and actual local TCP checks cover original skill controls, both portraits, ranked size, mana/cooldowns, damage gates, channel interruption, strict saves, summon expiry, AI skill learning, reconnect during teleport and saved missile sampling. The broad regression was validated in segments: the full entry's imported modules ran, and its main acceptance body was rerun after updating the projectile field contract. The corresponding source hash and exact execution method are recorded in `archmage-validation.json`; this is not a claim that the initial full-entry invocation exited successfully.

Native gameplay evidence is in `native-archmage-qa.json`; original shard geometry and particle-only projectile evidence is in `native-archmage-effects-qa.json`. These use the existing Release executable without an engine rebuild. Original Warcraft runtime parity, aura linger, detailed Blizzard wave timing/target-cap parity, Mass Teleport passenger eligibility, physical mouse input, audio listening and cross-machine LAN remain unverified. The complete Warcraft III campaigns, editor fidelity, classic TD/Dota maps and complete faction implementation remain unfinished.

Existing scene entity IDs are preserved; new Archmage effects are appended. The final gameplay stage runs in one owned native instance. Visual effects are checked separately at their visible animation phase. Native scalar comparisons allow the renderer's float precision; effect paths use exact original filename casing. Timing records distinguish startup, gameplay, effects and regression scope.
