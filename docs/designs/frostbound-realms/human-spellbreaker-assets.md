# Spell Breaker source effects

Author: MiYu

`scripts/import-frost-human-spellbreaker-effects.py` converts the original `SpellBreakerAttack.mdx`, `ControlMagicTarget.mdx`, `SpellStealMissile.mdx` and `SpellStealTarget.mdx` with their nine source textures. The signed receipt records 13 source files and 101 generated files, including asset and material metadata. Feedback is integrated into successful attack impacts and native effect playback. Spell Steal and Control Magic gameplay are pending, so the whole-catalog `runtimeIntegrated` flag remains false.

Feedback uses its original particle emitter: Birth lasts 2.1 seconds with 27 sampled frames and 39 sampled particles. Control Magic retains all four animated geometry parts; Birth lasts 2.2 seconds with 28 frames. Geometry and particles have separate conversion paths. The W3ModelViewer MIT notice covers the conversion library; it does not grant a license for Blizzard game assets.

Spell Steal retains all five animated missile geometry parts, both original particle emitters and their sampled billboard planes. Its Birth, Stand and Death clips contain 10, 9 and 9 sampled frames; Stand loops. The sampler records 105 particles and 107 billboard planes across those clips. The overhead target contains only particles: Birth lasts 2.966 seconds with 37 frames and 88 sampled particles. Only the target takes the particle-only conversion path. Missile geometry was compared against the original MDX pose sampler at 12 sample times (1,308 sampled vertices); maximum position error was `1.206176092338751e-08` engine units.

`python scripts/test-frost-human-spellbreaker-assets.py` verifies source and output hashes, generator and binary identities, GLB structures, metadata completeness, animation catalogs, independent byte-for-byte regeneration, modified-output protection and corrupt-source rejection. All 101 outputs reproduce, and all nine material sidecars are present. The test writes `human-spellbreaker-assets-validation.json`. Native Feedback playback passed the acceptance in `native-human-spellbreaker-qa.json`. Spell Steal and Control Magic native rendering, gameplay and original-runtime parity remain unverified.

## Gameplay evidence

The existing `human-sanctum.json` preserves the original `Afbk` and `Acmg` source rows. The [official Spell Breaker reference](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/spellbreaker.shtml) states that Feedback burns 20 mana from ordinary units or 4 from heroes, deals damage equal to the actual mana burned, and does not affect spell-immune units. Control Magic controls summoned units and Orc wards, costs mana in proportion to health, and requires its research.

Feedback physical armor treatment and the complete Control Magic charge/health formula require original-runtime measurements. The official reference specifies current hit points for Control Magic. Source values `Cost1=25`, `DataB1=0.45`, `DataC1=1` alone do not establish how the fixed charge and health adjustment participate in its full calculation.

The original `Asps` row specifies 75 mana, a 3-second cooldown, range 7 and receiver area 7. Its command card slot is 8, hotkey T, and source orders are `spellsteal`, `spellstealon` and `spellstealoff`. The official reference limits transfers to unit-cast spells and excludes hero spells; Unholy Frenzy, Cyclone and Anti-magic Shell require manual targeting. Remaining-duration handling, receiver selection and transfer timing still require original-runtime measurement before claiming parity. The existing Inner Fire, Invisibility, Slow, Faerie Fire, Frenzy and Lightning Shield states also have team, timer or source-attribution constraints that must remain valid after transfer.

[Warsmash PR 100](https://github.com/Retera/WarsmashModEngine/pull/100), head `ecff558794ca25b283fc1952a1d7955da780e986`, declares a MAGIC bonus for Feedback in `AttackReplacementPassivesNonItemLowPriority.json`, but `CBonusDamage.java` passes `primaryDamageType` to the armor calculation. This conflicting simulator implementation is insufficient to establish original Warcraft behavior.

An owned temporary map was generated outside the repository using a copied Amazonia map and replacement JASS. MPQ script roundtrip and JASS parsing against the installed libraries passed. Both absolute-path launch and relative-path launch from an owned runtime copy produced no measurement file. Windows observation recovery returned `GetCursorPos failed: 拒绝访问。 (0x80070005)`. Both owned processes exited after graceful close requests. Further original-runtime probing must establish script execution and result-file output before interpreting skill values.
