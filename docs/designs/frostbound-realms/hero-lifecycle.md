# Altar recruitment and hero revival

Author: MiYu

Skirmish altars offer the four existing hero classes individually. Ownership includes living heroes, fallen heroes and pending recruitment across all friendly altars. Each type may be recruited once; the second hero requires a tier-2 hall, the third requires tier 3, and the maximum is three. Recruitment reserves five supply for 55 seconds. A side with no owned or pending hero pays zero resources for its first recruitment; subsequent heroes cost 425 gold and 100 lumber. Queues store their paid costs so cancellation refunds the actual payment.

Fallen skirmish heroes remain in authoritative state with their original identity, experience, learned skills, skill points, attributes and six-slot inventory. They provide no vision, cannot receive orders and create no biological corpse. Their five supply remains reserved. An altar revives a selected fallen hero, with only one pending revival of that identity across all altars. Revival needs no additional supply or entity slot. A blocked spawn waits for a clear perimeter position; the hero returns with full health, 100 mana and cleared temporary effects. Spell and item cooldowns continue while dead. Rally orders apply after revival. Cancelling returns the revival payment; destruction loses the queue, leaving the hero available at another altar.

The gold calculation is `min(550, round(425 * (level + 3) / 10))`, with no lumber cost. Time is `min(110, 55 * level * 0.65)` seconds. These factors and limits follow Blizzard's [Hero Basics guide](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/heroes.shtml); the common 425/100/5 cost and 55-second recruitment time are also listed on the [Paladin page](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/paladin.shtml). The integer gold calculation uses nearest rounding. Native Warcraft executable parity for rounding and cooldown behavior has not been independently measured.

Three portrait shortcuts display living and fallen owned heroes. Clicking a living hero selects and centers it; clicking a fallen hero selects an available altar. Space cycles living heroes. Altar command cards use hero portraits and show class ownership, tier restrictions and revival costs. Own saved queues and reconnect states retain recruitment classes, paid costs and revival identities. Opponents receive neither production queues nor retained fallen hero state. Old saves migrate existing 300/80 hero queue refunds and remove obsolete hero corpse records. The wire protocol is 20, rejecting older clients with incompatible hero production rules.

Nearby heroes split kill experience in melee as well as MOBA. Ordinary weapon research no longer adds damage to hero attacks. The computer builds an altar for fallen heroes and prioritizes revival, then available hero recruitment, before flyers.

Validation uses `scripts/test-frost-hero-lifecycle.mjs`, the full `scripts/test-frostbound.mjs` rules and real TCP suite, `cargo test -p mengine-editor-host --test frost_sample`, and `scripts/qa-frost-hero-lifecycle.mjs`. Rules cover first-free payment, duplicate and tier limits, cancellation, multiple altars, lost altars, identity/items/skills, malformed and legacy saves, supply, capacity and split XP. TCP uses controlled authoritative production timers for queue/reconnect assertions. Native QA uses the real client, UI buttons, full production durations, save/load, an actual enemy attack, revival and all three portrait shortcuts in an isolated tier-3 practice field. It does not establish physical mouse, audio or cross-machine LAN acceptance.

Full Warcraft III recreation remains incomplete. Heroes still use the four original sample classes rather than all original faction heroes. Default melee resources, starting units, faction-specific attributes and abilities, first-hero Town Portal Scroll, tavern revival and original campaign/map content still need implementation. The default melee opening currently provides a hero at the start; the first-free recruitment path is exercised when no hero is owned.
