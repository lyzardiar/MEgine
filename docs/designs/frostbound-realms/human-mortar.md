# Original Workshop and Mortar source assets

Author: MiYu. This delivery preserves source data and renders original assets. Workshop production and Mortar gameplay remain to be integrated. The full Warcraft III objective remains active.

human-mortar.json preserves Workshop construction (1200 HP, 140 gold/140 lumber, 60 seconds; Keep and Blacksmith), Mortar Team training (360 HP, 180/70, three food, 32 seconds), original command slots/icons, source armor/dice/timing and both source weapons. The artillery weapon targets ground/debris/tree/wall/item/ward, with 25/150/250 source-unit splash bands at 1/.4/.1 damage. The second missile weapon targets structures without splash. Both retain range 1150, minimum range 250, cooldown 3.5 seconds, damage point 1 second and source projectile speed/arc. Source world conversion uses 100 game units per world unit.

Flare research costs 50/50 and 30 seconds, with Keep required. Its source ability preserves a 0.8-second effect delay, 15-second duration, 120-second cooldown, 1800-unit radius and detection type 3. Fragmentation Shards research costs 50/100 and 40 seconds, with Castle required. Its source targets are ground/enemy/neutral, and the official description limits additional damage to unarmored/medium armor. Source metadata and editor labels preserve the ordered full/medium/small radius/amount pairs 100/25, 275/18, 250/12. Both the MPQ and Blizzard reference have nonmonotonic radii. They do not prove overlap precedence or additional damage type; these remain explicit implementation questions. Normal artillery splash and enemy/neutral Fragmentation targets must be treated separately.

SourceAssets/HumanMortar retains raw SLK/text/BLP bytes with archive precedence and hashes. human-mortar-sources.json covers the data profiles and twelve original icons. human-mortar-effects-sources.json covers the original MortarMissile, FlareCaster and FlyingMachineImpact MDX files, complete texture dependencies, animated meshes/materials and sampled particles. Converters reuse the pinned W3ModelViewer revision and native binaries, whose hashes and source fingerprints are recorded. Original Blizzard game asset terms and converter MIT terms are separate.

The asset test checks source rows and normalized fields against the preserved MPQ bytes, validates output/generator hashes and effect dependencies, verifies animated GLB data and unique material sidecars, reproduces the data outputs and rejects edited outputs without changing any other file. All effect outputs and their receipt also reproduce byte-for-byte from the preserved signed source. Native preview shows original missile Stand/Death and Flare/Fragmentation Birth snapshots with original particles and visible mesh parts. Its current editor profile must report a rendered frame, with zero console errors and zero material pipeline rejections.

This native asset preview does not prove paid production, weapon targeting, friendly fire, moving-target artillery, spell visibility, save/load, networking, original executable solver parity, physical input or standalone player behavior. The existing generated game bundle is unchanged. The next step is to add source Workshop/Mortar gameplay and Flare to shared simulation, source client commands, strict saves and authoritative networking, then resolve and independently validate Fragmentation impact behavior before exposing that research.
