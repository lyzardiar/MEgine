# Original Dreadlord assets and profile

Author: MiYu. This milestone prepares original Dreadlord spells and the summoned Infernal for gameplay integration. The full Warcraft recreation remains active and incomplete. Ponytail is disabled; the shared asset library is read-only.

Imported ranked Carrion Swarm, Sleep, Vampiric Aura and Inferno field meanings, source AI builds and original cards. The Dreadlord starts with 600 HP, 270 maximum mana and 100 initial mana; uses strength for damage; has a 0.3-second cast point and 1.53-second cast backswing. The portable profile is game/dreadlord.js, with CommonJS/browser parity checked. It is not yet included in simulation.js or the generated client.

Swarm preserves damage 75/125/200, total budget 300/600/1000, travel distance 8, initial radius 1, final radius 3 and missile speed 11. Sleep preserves its 2-second initial stun separately from ordinary sleep 20/40/60 and hero sleep 5/10/15. Aura preserves 15/30/45% life steal and radius 9. Inferno preserves 1-second impact delay, 50 landing damage, ordinary/hero stun 4/2 seconds and 180-second summon lifetime.

The summon is ninf, with 1500 HP, regeneration 1 always, heavy armor 6, chaos melee average damage 54.5, cooldown 1.35, attack point 0.26, speed 3.2 and collision 0.48. Original model scale is 1; selection scale is 2.5, distinct from the Dreadlord's 1.5. ACmi magic damage factor 0, ANpi damage per interval 10/radius 2.2, and ACrk source records are retained. ANpi Dur=1 is not its summon lifetime. UnitData canSleep is automatic unit sleep, not evidence of immunity to the Sleep spell.

Twelve original effect bindings include nine animated geometry models, two particle-only effects and the Dreadlord's embedded emitters. The Infernal body, portrait, falling/landing model, Swarm geometry/ribbons, Sleep, aura and Immolation damage are imported with signed sources and reproducible cached converters. Two source portrait cameras preserve original coordinates and perspective. The Infernal portrait's opaque TeamGlow background belongs in its portrait viewport. Material flag 0x10 is TwoSided and 0x20 is Unfogged; the converter retains the correct source bits.

Validation: 405 fingerprinted files, 154 GUIDs, 12 native effects and 258 geometry pose samples passed. Offline outputs and receipts are byte-identical; modified outputs and corrupt source inputs are rejected. The profile/ranks/cards/AI/Infernal passives and browser parity passed. The final 77-entity native preview uses an independent source-camera portrait, verifies body dependencies, and reports zero console errors and shader rejections. The owned editor exited normally and its fixture/storage were cleaned. See dreadlord-assets-validation.json, native-dreadlord-assets-qa.json and dreadlord-native-source-effects.png.

Final asset validation took 11.978 seconds; the isolated native preview took 10.405 seconds, including 1.394 seconds to open the project and 7.775 seconds for snapshots and checks. No Rust build or full game scene launch was needed. Timing evidence and preparation retries are in dreadlord-assets-timing.json. These smaller previews cover source artwork; full-client acceptance remains required for gameplay integration.
