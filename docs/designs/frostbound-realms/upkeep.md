# Skirmish upkeep and production food

Author: MiYu

New skirmishes use `upkeepVersion: 1`. Food usage of 0–50, 51–80 and 81 or more selects 100%, 70% and 40% mining income respectively. The HUD shows the current tier and mining percentage beside food. Blizzard's [official upkeep guide](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/upkeep.shtml) specifies these thresholds and rates; the [basic guide](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/basics.shtml) specifies the 100-food cap. Gold carried by Human and Orc workers is taxed when delivered. Haunted and Entangled Mines tax each direct deposit. Mines lose the full harvested amount. Lumber, refunds, kill rewards, item sales, quest awards and MOBA passive income do not enter the mining settlement helper.

Living units consume food. An active training or altar revival queue head reserves its food once; later entries and research do not. Completion exchanges the reservation for the living unit, then attempts to reserve the next entry. A promoted head waits at its full timer until enough food is available. Losing a supply building does not cancel or suspend production that already reserved its food. Cancelling refunds the original payment and attempts to start the new head. Fallen heroes retain identity and inventory but release food; active revival reserves five food and reuses that identity.

Saves predating this marker restore as `upkeepVersion: 0`, retaining untaxed mining, all queued training reservations and the previous fallen-hero food convention. TD, MOBA and RPG remain untaxed. New saves validate the marker, the queue-head reservation and the full timer of waiting entries. Protocol 75 prevents an older peer from silently using different simulation rules. Enemy public state retains only its faction in the team record and hides production queues; the client calculates upkeep only for its own team.

Each deposit uses `floor(gross * percentage / 100)`. Standard 10/20-gold deposits are exact at every tier. A 5-gold tail in low upkeep credits 3. This rounding is an explicit project policy, not verified Warcraft executable behavior. Active-head and fallen-hero food semantics are supported by community references ([Food](https://liquipedia.net/warcraft/Food), [implementation notes](https://corepunch.github.io/open-realm/games/warcraft-3/food-and-upkeep/)); Blizzard's upkeep page does not establish those details. Original-executable parity for rounding and food transitions remains unaccepted.

Validation is recorded in `upkeep-validation.json` and `upkeep-native-qa.json`. The rule checks cover all four mining economies and tax boundaries, refunds and lumber, research/production/revival promotion, supply loss, malformed and legacy saves, deterministic continuation and the other three modes. Real TCP checks cover taxed deposits, gross depletion, reconnect, a death threshold transition and enemy privacy. Native Release QuickJS checks use the generated sample, actual F5/F10 input and rendered HUD at 1280×720 and 1920×1080. The additional HUD entity is appended after existing scene groups, preserving all 24,671 original entity records and IDs.

The complete historical entry now passes with 426 PASS records; fixture and native presentation acceptance is recorded in `client-acceptance-validation.json` and `client-acceptance-handoff.md`. These checks do not establish complete original-game or release parity.
